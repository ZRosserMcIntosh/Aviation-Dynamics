Switches and breakers

Weather

Below about ISA−5 the cloud is supercooled: ice builds on the pitot tube unless Pitot Heat is on. Watch the airspeed, and the truth table.

Replay

Live. The last 5 minutes are recorded.

CSV from avdemo instruments csv (native instrument recorder) replays a headless test flight on this panel.

Failures

Faults act on the physical model: a blocked pitot (drain open) bleeds to static, a blocked static port freezes the altimeter and VSI, a failed pump lets the gyros spin down.

# Aviation Dynamics

A physically grounded, data-driven flight simulation core in C++20 for the
São Paulo open world. It has no renderer and no game-engine dependency.
Aircraft are JSON definitions, and every parameter records where it came
from. The same core runs natively and in the browser as WebAssembly. It
drives the flight test bed at
[zrossermcintosh.com/sao-paulo/flying](https://zrossermcintosh.com/sao-paulo/flying).

**Status.**
- **Done:** Milestone 1 (single-engine piston foundation) and Milestone 2a
  (analog cockpit instrumentation).
- **Pulled forward from later milestones:**
  - turbofan jets;
  - a nonlinear lifting line;
  - the wind and weather field, and aircraft wake turbulence;
  - navigation across Brazil;
  - a blind-landing approach pilot.
- **Not yet:** helicopters, structural damage and multiplayer. They are on the
  [roadmap](docs/roadmap.md) and are not claimed here.

## The aircraft

| Aircraft | What it is | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| **Tupi T-180** | Fictional; flies as a Cessna 172S Skyhawk SP | Against its POH: stall, cruise, maximum speed, climb, take-off and landing, each within tolerance ([c172s-validation.md](docs/validation/c172s-validation.md)) |
| **Top G** | Fictional twin jet; flies as a Gulfstream G650ER | Against the G650ER's published figures: cruise at Mach 0.85 at FL450 with its fuel burn, approach and stall speeds, take-off (`tests/test_jet.cpp`) |
| **Phenom 300E, Citation CJ4, Learjet 45, CRJ700, 737-800** | Generated by `tools/jetgen` from each type's published figures | Engine lapse calibrated to the published maximum cruise, flap lift to a published Vref where one exists ([generated-jets.md](docs/validation/generated-jets.md)) |

The generated jets use each type's real figures: dimensions, weights, fuel,
engines (thrust, bypass ratio, TSFC), speed limits, flap detents, winglets,
T-tail, thrust reversers and speedbrakes. They are laid out on the Top G's
validated arrangement. Their fins are sized for each fuselage's yawing
moment, and they have a yaw damper.

## What works today

| Area | What is implemented | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 6-DoF | Rigid body in a local NED tangent frame on WGS-84, quaternion attitude, RK4 at a fixed 240 Hz step, floating-origin rebase | `tests/test_foundations.cpp` |
| Atmosphere | ISA-1976 with temperature and QNH deviation, moist density, one cloud layer with supercooled liquid water (pitot icing) | `tests/test_foundations.cpp` |
| Wind and weather | Log-law boundary layer over the surface roughness (city against airfield) with Ekman veer. MIL-HDBK-1797 turbulence, including the mechanical turbulence the wind makes. Gusts, microbursts, thermals, ridge lift and mountain waves, wind shifts and gust fronts, building and hangar wakes ([wind.md](docs/architecture/wind.md)) | `tests/test_wind.cpp` |
| Aerodynamics | Nonlinear lifting line on the wings (Phillips & Snyder), solved on full ±180° section polars, with ground effect, sweep theory and Fowler flaps. Winglets, spoilers on their real spans, gear and speedbrake drag where they are. Strip theory on the tail, prop slipstream and swirl, fuselage Munk moment, Korn/Lock drag rise | `tests/test_lifting_line.cpp`, `tests/test_components.cpp`, `tests/test_jet.cpp` |
| Wake turbulence | Each surface sheds its circulation into the air; it rolls up into vortex pairs that descend, drift with the wind, bounce off the ground and decay (P2P). Other aircraft fly through it | `tests/test_wake.cpp` |
| Propulsion | Blade-element propeller with swirl and tip compressibility; piston engine (manifold pressure, mixture, magnetos, starter, temperatures, oil pressure). Two-spool turbofans with spool lag, Mattingly thrust lapse, TSFC against Mach, windmilling and relight | `tests/test_components.cpp`, `tests/test_jet.cpp` |
| Jet systems | Retractable gear, speedbrakes, automatic ground spoilers, cascade thrust reversers, stick pusher, rudder limiter, a series yaw damper. AHRS heading, V-speeds from the trimmed stall speed | `tests/test_jet.cpp`, `tests/test_jetgen.cpp` |
| Systems | Load sheet (ramp, take-off, landing and zero-fuel weights, CG envelope, placards); fuel tanks, selector and shutoff, with line volume, starvation and leaks; battery, alternator and bus with named loads and breakers; engine-driven vacuum | `tests/test_systems_scenarios.cpp` |
| Ground | Spring-damper struts, combined-slip bristle tyres (one friction ellipse, sliding friction past the peak, cornering stiffness with load), wheel spin with torque-limited brakes that lock a light wheel, anti-skid where fitted, touchdown spin-up, a castoring nose wheel through a bungee linkage or steering by pedals or a rate-limited tiller, hard-point contacts. Wet runways (14 CFR 25.109), standing water and hydroplaning. The region's airports each at their own elevation; world tiles with terrain, surfaces and buildings (wingtip strikes) | `tests/test_ground_handling.cpp`, `tests/test_flight.cpp`, `tests/test_world.cpp` |
| Instruments | Pitot-static (IAS/CAS/EAS/TAS, position error, blockages, icing and pitot heat), VSI lag, vacuum gyros, turn coordinator and ball. A wet compass with airframe deviation and a swung correction card. Engine and system gauges, annunciators, transponder | `tests/test_instruments.cpp` |
| Navigation | VOR/LOC/GS receivers with radio horizon; the real ILSs at Congonhas, Guarulhos, Viracopos, Galeão and Brasília. 1,150 Brazilian airports (OurAirports runways). A GPS navigator: route legs, sequencing, Garmin-style CDI scaling, a 3° vertical path from the FAF, and generated approaches to any runway ([navigation.md](docs/architecture/navigation.md)) | `tests/test_destinations.cpp` |
| Blind landings | An instrument-only approach pilot (`scenarios::ApproachPilot`) flies the ILS or GPS approach with an autoland flare, decrab and rollout. All seven aircraft land in zero visibility within CAT III touchdown limits, calm and in a light crosswind | `tests/test_blind_landing.cpp`, [blind-landings.md](docs/validation/blind-landings.md) |
| Assists | Easy, Sport and Simulation, plus an auto-trim that holds straight and level. They act only through the pilot's controls; the physics is the same in all three ([ADR 0004](docs/decisions/0004-assistance-is-not-physics.md)) | `tests/test_systems_scenarios.cpp`, `tests/test_jet.cpp` |
| Browser | WebAssembly build (wasi-sdk, no exceptions or RTTI) with a C ABI. Streams the airflow, wake and body data the 3D site draws (streamlines, smoke, wind tunnel). Node smoke test, a self-contained 2D cockpit page, flight replay | `web/`, `web/smoke_test.mjs` |

## Build and run

```sh
cmake -S . -B build -G Ninja -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release
cmake --build build
./build/tests/avd_tests             # <!-- validation:begin tests -->226<!-- validation:end --> test cases
./build/tests/avd_tests "blind"     # a filter: the blind landings
./build/avdemo validate [--json f]  # the comparison tables against published data (172S POH, G650ER)
./build/tools/avdtrace --record     # record the golden traces (tests/golden/README.md)
./build/avdemo flight               # SBMT circuit: quick start, take-off, stall, pattern, landing
./build/avdemo instruments [csv]    # truth against display through a scripted flight
./build/avdemo starvation | shutdown | cockpit | bench
```

Options: `AVD_BUILD_TESTS`, `AVD_BUILD_TOOLS`, `AVD_WARNINGS_AS_ERRORS`, `AVD_FUZZ`
(libFuzzer target for the JSON parser and the aircraft loader, clang only),
`AVD_FUZZ_DRIVER` (the same target as a stand-alone mutation driver for any
compiler, e.g. under the gcc sanitizers).

The browser build needs [wasi-sdk](https://github.com/WebAssembly/wasi-sdk);
version 25 is tested.

```sh
WASI_SDK=/path/to/wasi-sdk web/build_wasi.sh   # web/dist: avd.wasm, avd.mjs and the seven aircraft JSONs
node web/smoke_test.mjs
node web/cockpit/build_page.mjs                # web/dist/cockpit.html (self-contained)
node web/cockpit/browser_test.mjs              # headless Chromium check (needs playwright)
```

Regenerating the jets (needs a WASM build in `web/dist`):

```sh
python3 tools/jetgen/jetgen.py                 # data/aircraft/{phenom300,cj4,learjet45,crj700,b738}.json
node tools/jetgen/calibrate.mjs                # re-fit engine lapse and flap lift (writes calibration.json)
node tools/jetgen/check.mjs                    # cruise, stall speeds, final approach, take-off
```


## Principles

The full statements are in [docs/architecture/overview.md](docs/architecture/overview.md).

- **No artificial limits.** Nothing caps speed, rate or load; the physics does.
- **Assistance is not physics.** Assists act only through the pilot's controls.
- **Scientific honesty.** Every aircraft parameter is tagged `published`,
  `calibrated`, `estimated` or `gameplay`, with its source. A disagreement with
  reference data is documented, not tuned away.
- **Independent model.** JSBSim (LGPL) is used only as a published reference,
  never as code ([ADR 0001](docs/decisions/0001-independent-flight-model.md)).

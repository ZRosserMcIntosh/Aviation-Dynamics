## Next milestone: 2b

The goal is to make the one aircraft trustworthy enough to build the rest on.

1. **Take-off roll.** ✅ Swirl and tip compressibility added, pitch returned
   to the published value, rolling resistance corrected: −19.9% → −12.5%.
   Swirl alone was refuted as the cause. Now within the symmetric ±20%
   tolerance; the "known discrepancy" note and the widened test bound are
   gone. Possible refinement: the lift-off and transition (the air segment
   is about 30% long) against flight-test data.
2. **Landing braking technique.** ✅ A pilot braking model (delay, ramp,
   sustained effort) gives −18.7%, within tolerance. Originally planned as:
   add a pilot braking model (ramp, hold, anti-skid by feel), compare the
   landing roll at POH technique, and keep the
   tyre-limited result as a separate test.
3. **São Paulo World ground query.** 🟡 The integration layer is done:
   `TiledWorldGround` (terrain heightfield, surface classes, extruded building
   footprints, `solidAt` wall contact for the hard points) and the
   `avd-world-tile/1` format, documented in
   [architecture/world-ground.md](architecture/world-ground.md) and tested on
   a synthetic tile. **Blocked on data:** the real terrain and building
   geometry lives in the SaoPauloWorld project, which is not reachable from
   this repository. Next: an exporter there, then the SBMT circuit over real
   terrain.
4. **Trimmed approach spawn in the browser.** ✅ The trim solver is pure
   (`src/scenarios/trim.cpp`) and runs in WASM. Final approach and cruise
   start trimmed and hands-off, on the ILS.
5. **Cockpit follow-ups:** ✅ compass deviation from the airframe's own field,
   with a correction card generated by a compass swing; ✅ pitot icing in
   supercooled cloud, cleared by pitot heat; ✅ flight replay in the web
   cockpit, including native `InstrumentRecorder` CSV.
6. **CI.** Keep every job green: Linux, macOS, Windows, sanitizers, WASM
   and the headless-browser cockpit test.

Exit criteria:

- every POH comparison is within its tolerance without a "known discrepancy"
  note;
- the SBMT circuit flies on real terrain;
- CI is green on all platforms.

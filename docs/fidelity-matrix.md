# Fidelity matrix

What is modelled, how, and how far it is validated, as of October 2026. Rows
cover the master brief's areas, including the ones not started.

Legend: ✅ implemented and tested · 🟡 partial or simplified · ⛔ not started.

| Area | Status | Model today | Validation | Main gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rigid-body 6-DoF | ✅ | RK4, 240 Hz, quaternion, WGS-84 local frame, rebase | Determinism, convergence, rebase tests | Rotating-earth terms (Coriolis) ignored: fine below Mach 1 at this scale |
| Atmosphere | ✅ | ISA-1976, ΔT, QNH, humidity, one cloud layer with liquid water | Table checks against ISA; icing tests | Precipitation; airframe icing (wing, prop, induction) not modelled |
| Wind and turbulence | ✅ | Log-law boundary layer with surface roughness, Ekman veer, winds aloft; MIL-HDBK-1797 turbulence (height-dependent, anisotropic), mechanical turbulence from the actual wind, severity classes, 1-cosine gusts, Oseguera–Bowles microbursts, Allen thermals (stream function, life cycle, drift), Queney ridge lift and mountain waves, convective σ ([architecture/wind.md](architecture/wind.md)) | `tests/test_wind.cpp`: MIL σ/L values, realised RMS and anisotropy, gradient consistency | See the weather-field row |
| Weather field | ✅ | `SimpleWeather`: roughness patches (city vs airfield), wind stations interpolated in space and time, moving wind shifts and gust fronts, obstacle (building/hangar) wakes; São Paulo surroundings for SBMT | `tests/test_wind.cpp` spatial cases; web smoke test flies a microburst on final | No pressure/temperature fields (fronts shift wind only); internal boundary layers at roughness edges not modelled; trapped lee waves and rotors absent |
| Wake turbulence (aircraft) | ✅ | `Wake` (`avd/wake.hpp`): each surface's trailing-edge circulation shed into the air as vortex-ring gates (unsteady-VLM style; starting and stopping vortices included), Betz roll-up of same-sign runs over the Spreiter–Sacks distance with the sheet spiralling in, 2-D pair transport (mutual induction, ground images, local wind and its gradient), Lamb–Oseen cores with Squire growth, two-phase decay after Holzäpfel's P2P (T₂* = 0.8 ε*^−3/4); regularised Biot–Savart field; `WakeField` puts wakes in the air an aircraft samples (span-averaged gradient) | `tests/test_wake.cpp`: segment field Γ/2πr, pair descent Γ₀/2πb₀ (±3 %), image-vortex levelling and spreading, Squire core growth, decay vs turbulence, C172: b₀/b 0.79, ρVΓ₀b₀ = 1.03 W, sink rate; heavy-pair encounter rolls a C172 past 25° in 1 s | Pairs evolve in 2-D per gate (no Crow instability or vortex rings, no rebound off the ground, no stratification); swept tapered wings get strip theory's loading (the Top G sheds b₀/b ≈ 0.5 until the lifting line, step 4); only the host's own aircraft sheds |
| Wing aerodynamics | ✅ | Nonlinear lifting line (Phillips & Snyder 2000) on the wings: horseshoe vortices solved together by Newton on the full section polars, ground images with the wake trailing along the ground, artificial viscosity where separated (Chattot 2004), control effects carried spanwise over a chord; winglets as canted panels. Sections in the plane normal to the span (simple sweep theory: α, slope, α₀ and clmax mapped by cos Λ), ±180° polar with Viterna's finite-wing CDmax, Fowler chord extension, Oswald remainder (1/e − 1)/(πAR)·CL² for interference and viscous drag due to lift; tails by strip theory (Helmbold slope), downwash lag; trimmable horizontal stabiliser (jets) | Classical results: Prandtl elliptic CLα within 1.2 %, e 1.02; Glauert rectangular AR 8 CLα 4.96, e 0.97; Clp −0.533 vs −0.525; swept-wing tip loading and ground-effect induced-drag cut; a winglet −13 % induced drag. Aircraft: C172 stall speeds +0.3 %/−0.1 % with the published NACA 2412 clmax (clean unfitted); Top G swept-wing aerodynamic centre ~36 % MAC (vortex-lattice reference 32 %), tip stall and pitch-up held off by its AoA-computer pusher | Steady (no dynamic stall yet); control points on the quarter chord (no Weissinger 3/4-chord correction: swept roots slightly off); post-stall solutions regularised, not validated; tails strip theory |

| Slipstream | 🟡 | Axial momentum plus swirl angle on washed strips | Tail-down take-off roll | Simplified contraction |
